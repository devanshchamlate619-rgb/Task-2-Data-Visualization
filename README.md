# Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

## Task 2: Data Visualization and Storytelling

### Objective

The objective of this project is to create visualizations that communicate business insights effectively using the Superstore sales dataset.

## Tool Used

- Microsoft Power BI Desktop

## Dataset

The Superstore dataset contains sales-related information including:

- Sales
- Profit
- Quantity
- Discount
- Category
- Region
- Product
- Order Date
- Customer and geographical information

## Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard presents:

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Profit Margin
- Sales by Category
- Profit by Category
- Sales by Region
- Top 10 Products by Sales
- Monthly Sales Trend
- Year and Region filters

## Key Insights

1. Total sales are approximately 2.30M.
2. Total profit is approximately 286.40K.
3. Overall profit margin is approximately 12.47%.
4. Technology is the leading category in both sales and profit.
5. The West region contributes the highest sales.
6. Sales show strong increases during September and November.
7. The Top 10 Products visualization highlights the products contributing most strongly to sales.

## Data Storytelling

The dashboard uses appropriate charts and KPI cards to present the overall business performance and identify important patterns in sales, profit, products, categories, regions, and monthly trends.

A separate Key Insights & Summary page was created to communicate the major business findings clearly.

## Project Files

- `Superstore.csv` – Dataset
- `Superstore_Sales_Analysis.pbix` – Power BI project
- `Superstore_Sales_Visualization_Report.pdf` – Final visual report

## Conclusion

The Power BI dashboard transforms the Superstore sales data into an interactive visual report that highlights important business trends and supports data-driven interpretation.
